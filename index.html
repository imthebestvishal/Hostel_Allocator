— Allocation Policy

How Seats Are Allocated

Our algorithm follows university policy strictly — fair, automated, and transparent.

I

Disabled / Handicapped

Top priority for all physically challenged or specially-abled students.

II

Outside Delhi Category

Students from outside Delhi, ranked by 12th marks merit list.

III

Delhi — Parents Transferred

Delhi students whose parents are posted outside Delhi (Govt./PSU).

IV

Delhi — Distance Based

Remaining Delhi students, ranked by distance from East Delhi Campus.

V

Waiting List

Students not allocated initially. Allotted as vacancies arise.
